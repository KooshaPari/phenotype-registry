# Known issues and decision gates

- Research PR samples are selective. They establish observed acceptance, not a measured overall acceptance rate.
- Stars were observed September 4 Pacific / September 5 UTC 2026. Historical growth was not collected.
- Most maintainers' agency backgrounds, employment histories, and solo status are unverified.
- The earlier developer list was selected on institutional role and is superseded for this strategy.
- Current issue/PR overlap may invalidate a proposal; recheck before coding.
- CCProxy has older/narrow acceptance evidence; responsiveness gates all investment beyond reconnaissance.
- Current CCProxy planning inspection found application version 2.0.0, license AGPL-3.0-or-later, and a coverage ratchet; open PR #16 concerns the architecture. Its earlier quick model-config merge is insufficient evidence of current low review burden. All five proposals require current maintainer scope agreement.
- DeskcommCRM has active PR triage and contribution reconciliation; recent externally authored work may be extracted into maintainer follow-ups. Track attribution and avoid implementing already reconciled changes.
- Jayjay source declares BUSL-1.1 in its GPUI package. Contribution value does not establish commercial reuse rights.
- Broad feature acceptance is less established for gpt-load and Atlas than for KeiRouter or token-monitor.
- Runtime tests of proposed changes have not been run: implementation is outside this request.
- No statement that a project has little review implies that unsafe or poorly verified contributions are appropriate.
