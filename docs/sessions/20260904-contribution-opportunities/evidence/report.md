Observed September 4, 2026 Pacific / September 5 UTC. 54 unique repositories screened, all 100-50,000 stars at their recorded snapshots. Ten selected below. It does not classify people as amateurs or infer agency/enterprise backgrounds.

## Corrections to previous answers

The prior shortlist over-weighted corporate/standards prestige and technical adjacency. Small star count alone does not establish outside access, and institutional reviewer biographies do not match the requested Diego-like builder opportunity. The prior answer also mixed star bands and supplied non-auditable citation tokens; its counts and claims should be replaced by this live evidence. Diego career and original project-quality descriptions are user context, not independently established findings. Current OmniRoute outside merges are documented in routing evidence; current unmerged closures disprove literal acceptance of every PR.

The key signal is substantive non-owner PRs actually landing with manageable iteration, including maintainer willingness to finish changes while preserving credit. CONTRIBUTOR/NONE is a public proxy, not proof of employment independence; COLLABORATOR and bot merges are not newcomer evidence. Zero public comments does not prove no private review. Merge latency is wall time, not labor. Samples are not acceptance probabilities.
