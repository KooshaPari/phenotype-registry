# PhenoKits — Per-Module Boundary Disposition

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Branch:** `docs/boundary-disposition-2026-06-16`
**Charter:** [phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md)
**Doctrine:** [boundary-shaping.md:7-19](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) — every module/crate/folder is assigned one of:

1. **DECOMPOSE** — split into tightly-scoped domain repos
2. **ABSORB** — move to a better existing repo (history-preserving); reverse = infra-generic stuff (tests/CI/governance) hoists to **HexaKit scaffolding-gen**
3. **DYNAMIC-KEEP** — too small to own a repo → dynamic-install monorepo (e.g. `phenoShared`)

**No deletions.** A stub, empty, or broken module is still assigned an owner.

## 2. Recommended end-state for PhenoKits

The PhenoKits repository, as it stands on 2026-06-16, is a **shared-artifact monorepo** that bundles 12 categories of cross-org artifacts (templates, configs, libs, governance, security, observability, docs, scripts, schemas, policies, credentials, secrets) plus a `hexagon/` curation and a `HexaKit/` submodule. The boundary-shaping charter pre-assessment at [boundary-shaping.md:34](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) is: **Decompose**.

The recommended end-state is:

1. **PhenoKits as a repo is archived** after every asset has been relocated to a real owner. The archive step is the *last* step, not the first, per charter [boundary-shaping.md:42-43](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md): *"archive emptied shells only after relocation"*.
2. **Scaffolding layer concentrates in HexaKit** (`KooshaPari/HexaKit`), which is reframed per [boundary-shaping.md:36](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) from "hexagonal toolkit currently holds libs" into **templates / scaffolding-gen, not a lib holder**. HexaKit owns: `templates/`, `configs/`, `secrets/`, `security/`, `scripts/`, `governance/` (standards), `policies/` (CI gates), `hexagon/`, repo-meta files (`.editorconfig`, `.gitignore`, `Cargo.*`, `pytest.ini`, `pyproject.toml`, `Taskfile.yml`, `justfile`, `cliff.toml`, `clippy.toml`, `deny.toml`, `rustfmt.toml`, `.pre-commit-config.yaml`, `trufflehog.yml`, `renovate.json5`), `.claude/`, `.devcontainer/`, `.github/` (CODEOWNERS, dependabot, workflows, ISSUE_TEMPLATE, PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE), and the top-level governance files (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `STATUS.md`, `COMPARISON.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `CITATION.cff`, `CODEOWNERS`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `FUNDING.yml`, licenses). Tests that *validate scaffolding* (rows 21) move to HexaKit.
3. **Domain SDKs receive the language-specific libs**:
   - `KooshaPari/phenotype-python-sdk` (umbrella) hosts `pheno-cli-builder`, `pheno-cli-kit`, `phenotype-id`, `phenotype-py-kit` (shared base) — per charter [boundary-shaping.md:34](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) and [boundary-shaping.md:38](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md).
   - `phenotype-logging` and `phenotype-testing` may live in `phenotype-python-sdk` (as `observability/` and `testing/` slices) or be routed to **`KooshaPari/PhenoObservability`** and **`KooshaPari/TestingKit`** per charter [boundary-shaping.md:26](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) — pending the per-domain SDK's actual scope.
   - `KooshaPari/phenotype-ts-sdk`, `KooshaPari/phenotype-go-sdk` (or `phenotype-go-sdk`), and the Rust split (driven by the `pheno` 170K-LOC assessment) absorb the other `libs/*` sub-workspaces.
4. **Authvault** (`AuthKit` → `Authvault` per [boundary-shaping.md:26](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md)) absorbs `credentials/`.
5. **Schemas** split: canonical types → a new `phenotype-types` repo; per-language codegen output → the per-language SDK; OpenAPI/Protobuf → their owning domain repos.
6. **Policies** split: CI gates and branch-protection → HexaKit; OPA rego and SOC2/GDPR controls → a new `phenotype-policies` (or `phenotype-governance`) repo.
7. **Observability** splits inside `PhenoObservability`: yaml configs → HexaKit-generated; collector/SDK code → `PhenoObservability` SDK.
8. **Stubs and cross-cutting bits that are too small to own a repo** (`Tracera/`, `kitty-specs/`, `worklogs/`, `docs/AGENT_PATTERNS.md`, `docs/adr/`, `docs/journeys/`, `docs/operations/`, `docs/reference/`, `docs/guides/`, `docs/migrations/`, `docs/tests/`, `docs/RESTRUCTURING_*`, `src/`) route to **`phenoShared`** as the dynamic-install monorepo, per charter [boundary-shaping.md:28](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) and [boundary-shaping.md:37](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md).
9. **`HexaKit/` submodule pointer**: the submodule entry stays as-is. Once HexaKit itself reframes per [boundary-shaping.md:36](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md), the existing `.gitmodules` link to `KooshaPari/HexaKit.git` is the correct home for all scaffolding — and the PhenoKits-side `templates/`, `configs/`, etc. flow into it. The submodule does not get "deleted" from PhenoKits; PhenoKits itself eventually archives once every *other* asset has been relocated.

### Sequence of follow-up work

The charter mandates history-preserving moves (not delete-and-recreate) at [boundary-shaping.md:42-43](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md):

1. **PR-set 1 — HexaKit absorbs scaffolding**: `git mv` (or history-preserving equivalents) of `templates/`, `configs/`, `secrets/`, `security/`, `scripts/`, `governance/standards/`, `policies/{github,compliance}/`, `hexagon/`, all repo-meta, `.github/`, `.claude/`, `.devcontainer/`, repo-governance top-level files, and `tests/` into `KooshaPari/HexaKit`. Tracked as one history-preserving relocation PR per asset group, with `git filter-repo` or `git subtree split` for history if needed.
2. **PR-set 2 — Domain SDK splits**: for each Python lib (4a-4g), a `phenotype-python-sdk` PR. For Rust/TS/Go, route to those language's assessment PRs.
3. **PR-set 3 — Domain SDKs receive their domains**: `Authvault` gets `credentials/`; `PhenoObservability` gets the observability SDK code; `TestingKit` gets the testing helper; `phenotype-types` gets the canonical schemas.
4. **PR-set 4 — `phenoShared` collects too-small bits**: `Tracera/`, `kitty-specs/`, `worklogs/`, cross-cutting docs.
5. **Final — PhenoKits archive**: only after PR-sets 1-4 are merged. The shell becomes a redirect / archive notice.

### PhenoKits is not a "delete-on-sight" candidate

Per charter doctrine [boundary-shaping.md:9-19](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md): *"A stub / empty / broken / unused / incomplete module is **not** a delete candidate by default. On-paper-good boundaries (e.g. a "Testing Boundary") still deserve an owner."* This document assigns every top-level entry an owner (35 rows above). Even the `Tracera/` empty stub (row 18) gets `phenoShared` as its dynamic-install home, with a future claim by a real domain repo.

## 3. Open questions for the boundary-shaping working group

These decisions need human input before the follow-up PRs can be filed:

1. **Is `phenotype-python-sdk` the canonical home for `pheno-cli-builder` + `pheno-cli-kit`, or should there be a separate `pheno-cli` SDK?** Charter [boundary-shaping.md:38](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) calls it an umbrella; if CLI is a real domain, it might want its own repo.
2. **`phenotype-logging` and `phenotype-testing`** — are they per-language slices of the python-sdk umbrella, or do they belong in `PhenoObservability` and `TestingKit` respectively (charter [boundary-shaping.md:26](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md) lists both as domain SDKs)?
3. **Is `phenotype-types` a real repo, or do per-language types live in the per-language SDKs and the canonical types live in `phenotype-registry`?**
4. **Policies**: do OPA rego + SOC2/GDPR controls get a new `phenotype-policies` repo, or do they fold into `phenotype-registry` (the governance/registry home)?
5. **`phenokits-commons` (referenced in charter [boundary-shaping.md:39](../../../phenotype-registry/docs/rationalization/boundary-shaping.md))** — does it correspond to any of the modules in this assessment (likely `pheno-py-kit` shared base, or a cross-cutting module), or is it a separate concern that lives elsewhere?
